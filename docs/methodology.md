# MarketPulse — Data & Methodology

## Project Context

MarketPulse is a self-initiated portfolio case study designed to explore the type of consumer, marketplace and sales analysis used in commercial decision-making.

It is not an official adidas project and does not use confidential or internal adidas data.

## Data Approach

The project uses a hybrid data approach combining:

1. Publicly available adidas company and market benchmarks
2. Derived assumptions used to structure the analytical model
3. Synthetic transaction-level data created specifically for this portfolio case study

Because granular transaction-level adidas data is not publicly available, a synthetic dataset of 30,000 transactions was created covering January 2025 to September 2026.

The synthetic data is designed to support realistic analytical scenarios rather than reproduce actual adidas internal performance.

## Analysis Scope

The model covers:

- 5 markets: Germany, United Kingdom, United States, China and United Arab Emirates
- 5 categories: Running, Football, Originals, Training and Outdoor
- 3 channels: Online DTC, Retail DTC and Wholesale
- Consumer segments: Women, Men and Unisex
- Multiple product franchises including Adizero, Ultraboost, Samba, Gazelle, Predator, F50 and Terrex

## Analytical Framework

MarketPulse follows a simple decision-support framework:

**SIGNAL → SO WHAT? → ACTION**

The dashboard first identifies a performance signal, interprets its potential commercial meaning and then proposes an area for further investigation or action.

Measures include:

- Revenue and revenue growth
- Gross margin
- Sell-through
- Consumer interest
- Year-over-year comparisons
- Opportunity gap
- Opportunity prioritization

## Modelled Commercial Scenarios

Several scenarios were intentionally embedded in the synthetic dataset to demonstrate how the dashboard can surface different commercial questions.

### Consumer demand vs. commercial performance

A Germany / Women / Running / Online DTC scenario demonstrates consumer-interest growth developing faster than revenue growth.

### Growth vs. profitability

A US / Originals / Online DTC scenario demonstrates strong commercial growth alongside comparatively lower margins.

### Channel momentum

A UK scenario demonstrates different growth trajectories between Online DTC and Wholesale.

### Demand vs. availability

A China / Outdoor / Terrex Agravic scenario demonstrates high consumer interest and sell-through, creating an inventory-pressure signal.

These scenarios are analytical demonstrations and should not be interpreted as actual adidas performance.
